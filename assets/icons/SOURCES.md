# Tool icon sources

The 20 brand SVGs in this folder come from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon) at commit `7330accdbc47e2dc0c19789a48533c4a3c50fe58`. The Devicon license is included in `LICENSE.devicon`.

The files `sql.svg`, `rest-api.svg`, `azure-functions.svg`, `databricks.svg`, `azure-blob-storage.svg`, `ocr.svg`, `javafx.svg`, and `mvc.svg` are simple pictograms made for this site. They represent concepts or services and are not official brand logos.
